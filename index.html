01THE WHY

A front row seat to the quantum future.

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global celebration made local. This year, we are exploring A Decade of Quantum on the Cloud through one energetic day of talks, hands-on work, and real conversations.

Whether you are opening your first notebook or already shipping experiments, there is a place for you here. Come curious. Leave connected.

08GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

More details will land here as the event takes shape.

Who can attend?+

Anyone curious about quantum computing. Students, developers, educators, researchers, designers, and first-timers are all welcome.

Is prior quantum knowledge required?+

No. The day is designed with clear on-ramps for beginners, alongside deeper sessions for experienced builders.

Will the event be free?+

Yes, the event is completely free for everyone. No registration or participation fee.

Do I need a laptop?+

A laptop is recommended for the hands-on workshop. We will share account and software setup instructions before the event.

Where can I get updates?+

Join our Community On Discord & stay updated, stay connected, stay ahead!
